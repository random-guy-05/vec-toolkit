## Title
VEC P3 Survival Guide - illustrated final-phase guide for 20 October

## What it is, in a line
A four-page illustrated P3 transition and final-submission guide.

## What it is, and who it helps
A four-page illustrated final-phase guide built specifically for the 20 October P3 transition. It shows what becomes available for retraining, what remains hidden, the task-specific hidden test targets, the two-official-submissions-per-board rule, unlimited format checks, the non-withdrawable nature of official submissions, and a practical final workflow from released validation data to frozen upload artifacts. The finished resource ships as PDF plus editable SVG/PNG pages with source notes and accessibility text. It is designed for every active team approaching P3, when a single wrong-file or stale-training assumption is much more costly than in P2.

## How someone else uses it
Open the repository and use the ready-made PNG/PDF assets, or edit the source SVG files. No Challenge data, sign-in, or Python environment is required to use the finished visuals.
